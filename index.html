Our Compass

"Educate. Agitate. Organize." — Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's call to collective action is our operating manual. We educate communities on the science and safety of donation, we agitate against the stigma and myths that keep people away, and we organize the camps, the registries, and the phone trees that get blood to a patient in time — from our head office in Jalandhar to donor networks across India, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom.

We are not a hospital and we are not a blood bank — we are the volunteer network in between. When a family anywhere in our network needs a rare unit at 2 a.m., our job is to already know who has it, and to have earned enough trust that they pick up the phone.

How it works

From registration to recovery, in four steps

The process is deliberately simple — because in an emergency, simple is what saves time.

01

Register

Share your blood group, contact number, and city. You join the registry that hospitals and families call in an emergency.

02

Get matched

When a request comes in, we check eligibility, distance, and last donation date before we ever pick up the phone.

03

Donate safely

A quick health screening, a supervised donation of about 10 minutes, and rest with refreshments — every time.

04

Be honoured

Frequent donors are recognised at our camps — a small thank-you for a decision that isn't small at all.
